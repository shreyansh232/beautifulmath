# Sources - Transformations and symmetry

## Math and history
- MacTutor: Felix Klein (1849-1925); Erlangen appointment 1872; https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Klein/
- Klein, Vergleichende Betrachtungen über neuere geometrische Forschungen (1872), the Erlangen program: geometry as invariants of a transformation group. English translation: arXiv:0807.3161; https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3161
- Note: prepared as inaugural program publication; commonly described as not delivered as a ceremonial speech (Baez / historical notes).
- Euclid Elements Book I congruence tradition (Joyce): https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/java/elements/bookI/bookI.html
- Standard modern classification flavor: plane isometries generated by translations, rotations, reflections (and glide reflections in full classification).

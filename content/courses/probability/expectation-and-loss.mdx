## First principles

For a discrete random variable $X$ with values $x_i$ and probabilities $p_i$,

$$
\mathbb{E}[X] = \sum_i x_i p_i
$$

It is the center of mass of the distribution.

<DistributionDiagram />

Linearity: $\mathbb{E}[aX + bY] = a\mathbb{E}[X] + b\mathbb{E}[Y]$ always (no independence needed).

## Variance (brief)

$\mathrm{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}[(X - \mathbb{E}X)^2]$ measures spread.

## Loss and risk

A loss $\ell(y, \hat{y})$ scores a mistake. **Risk** is $\mathbb{E}[\ell(Y, f(X))]$. Machine learning estimates risk with an average over samples.

## Real world

A fair game has expected net gain near zero. Casinos set rules so their expectation is positive.

## AI / ML bridge

MSE, cross-entropy, hinge loss: different geometries of error. Optimization (calculus) + linear maps (linear algebra) + expectation (this lesson) meet in training.

## First principles

Conditional probability of $A$ given $B$ (with $P(B) > 0$):

$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}
$$

Restrict attention to the world where $B$ happened, then weigh $A$ inside that world.

## Bayes' rule

$$
P(H \mid E) = \frac{P(E \mid H) P(H)}{P(E)}
$$

Posterior from likelihood times prior, normalized by evidence.

## Base rates matter

A rare disease and a mostly-accurate test can still yield many false alarms. Intuition fails without Bayes. Always track the prior.

## Real world

Email: $P(\text{spam} \mid \text{words})$. Medicine: $P(\text{condition} \mid \text{marker})$.

## AI / ML bridge

Training data estimates likelihood-like terms. Regularization and priors encode beliefs before seeing the batch. Calibration compares predicted probabilities to empirical frequencies.

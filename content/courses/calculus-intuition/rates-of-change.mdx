## First principles

Average rate of change of $y$ between $x = a$ and $x = b$ is:

$$
\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}
$$

That is rise over run on a secant.

<SlopeDiagram />

**Instantaneous** rate is what that ratio approaches as $b$ approaches $a$ (when the limit exists). That limiting slope is the derivative idea.

## Slow down: average first, instant next

Average rate is honest over an interval. Instantaneous rate is the limiting dream of smaller intervals. When the dream converges, we call it the derivative. When a corner blocks the dream, the derivative refuses to exist there.

## Why Newton cared

Planets do not move at constant speed along simple school lines. Fluxions described flowing quantities. The same pattern appears in any smooth change.

## Intuition without formalism terror

Zoom in on a smooth curve. Locally it looks like a straight line. That line's slope is the rate right there.

## Real world

If distance is $s(t)$, velocity is the rate $s'(t)$. If velocity itself changes, acceleration is the rate of velocity.

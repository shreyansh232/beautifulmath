## Math and history
- MacTutor al-Battani: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Al-Battani/
  - Zīj; observational corrections to Ptolemaic parameters; trigonometric tools in Islamic astronomical practice.
- MacTutor Ptolemy: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Ptolemy/
- MacTutor Trigonometric functions history: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Trigonometric_functions/
  - Chords (Hipparchus, Ptolemy) to half-chords/sines (Indian mathematicians; Islamic development).
- Standard principal ranges for arcsin/arccos/arctan; law of sines and cosines (textbook forms).
- Do not claim al-Battani alone invented modern law of sines; place in a long relay (chords → sines → systematic plane/spherical laws, later including figures such as Nasīr al-Dīn al-Tūsī in standard histories).

# Sources - SVD and PCA

## Math and history
- MacTutor Pearson: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Pearson/
- SVD lineage: Beltrami and Jordan (19th c.), later Sylvester, Schmidt, Weyl; surveys such as G. W. Stewart, "On the Early History of the Singular Value Decomposition," SIAM Review 35 (1993).
- Low-rank approximation: Schmidt (integral operators); Eckart-Young (matrices); Mirsky generalization (standard attribution caution in literature).
- PCA: Pearson and later Hotelling; computed via eigen-decomposition of covariance or SVD of centered data (standard).

## AI-ML
- PCA dimensionality reduction; truncated SVD; honest boundary versus nonlinear deep representations.
- SVD for stable least squares / pseudoinverse (standard numerical linear algebra).

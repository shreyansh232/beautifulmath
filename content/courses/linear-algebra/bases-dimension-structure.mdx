## First principles

Vectors $\mathbf{v}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{v}_k$ are **linearly independent** if the only solution to

$$
a_1\mathbf{v}_1 + \cdots + a_k\mathbf{v}_k = \mathbf{0}
$$

is all $a_i = 0$. No vector in the set is redundant.

A **basis** of a space is an independent spanning set: every vector has unique coordinates relative to that basis.

**Dimension** is the number of vectors in any basis (a theorem says they all agree).

## Why this matters

Coordinates are not the object. The space is. Change basis and coordinates change, but geometric relationships (when properly expressed) persist. Noether's broader algebraic spirit is exactly this: track the structure that survives rewriting.

## Rank (preview)

The column space dimension of a matrix is its rank: how many independent output directions it can hit.

## Real world

Three mutually perpendicular unit axes basis ordinary space. Four legs of a table that are coplanar constraints show dependence in physical disguise.

## AI / ML bridge

If a layer's weight matrix has low rank, it bottlenecks information. Embeddings live in a chosen dimension: enough axes to separate concepts, not so many that you drown in noise.

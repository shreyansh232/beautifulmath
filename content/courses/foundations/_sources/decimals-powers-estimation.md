# Sources - Decimals, powers, and estimation

## Math and history
- MacTutor: Simon Stevin (1548-1620) - elementary thorough European account of decimal fractions in De Thiende / La Disme (1585); aimed at stargazers, surveyors, merchants, etc.; earlier Arabic and Chinese decimal-fraction uses acknowledged; https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Stevin/
- MacTutor topic: The real numbers: Pythagoras to Stevin; https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Real_numbers_1/
- English translation Disme (Robert Norton, 1608) and later influence on decimal currency advocacy (Jefferson / dime note per MacTutor).
- Stevin notation used circled place markers, not modern decimal point; point notation developed via later writers (Clavius, Napier lineage per MacTutor).

## Mathematician vignette
- Stevin: method for practical readers; decimal advocacy for coinage/weights/measures; engineer-advisor context (MacTutor).

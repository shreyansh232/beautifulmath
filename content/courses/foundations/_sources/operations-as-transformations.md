# Sources - Operations as transformations

## Math
- Knill arithmetic notes: addition as combining collections; multiplication as repeated addition for wholes, with commutativity via array/area insight; https://people.math.harvard.edu/~knill/teaching/mathe320_2021/handouts/01-arithmetic.pdf
- Standard first-principles framing: operations as maps on a set (N → Z → Q), invertibility of +/− and ×/÷ (nonzero).

## Mathematician vignette
- MacTutor: Srinivasa Ramanujan (1887–1920) - notebooks (~3,300 results), influence of Carr’s *Synopsis*, correspondence with Hardy; https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Ramanujan/
- Berndt, “Living with Ramanujan for 40 years,” Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A - notebooks as personal compilation, proof sketches rare; https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsta.2018.0437
- IMSc notes on notebook history (1903–1914); https://www.imsc.res.in/~rao/ramanujan/images/KSRchap3.pdf

## Real world
- Scaling recipes, currency conversion, unit changes - multiplication as scale factor; undoing with division.

## AI / ML bridge
- Affine transforms, weight matrices as linear maps (foreshadowing; keep honest at foundations level): a weight multiplies an input - the same “scale and combine” idea. Loss gradients later reverse through those multiplications (chain rule preview, not calculus yet).

## Honesty notes
- Ramanujan is used for work ethic / passion for structure of numbers and operations, not as the inventor of arithmetic.
- Keep ML bridge at “operations are the verbs models apply,” not claiming he anticipated neural nets.

## Story notes (The Joy of X)
- Steven Strogatz, *The Joy of X* (2012); companion site https://www.stevenstrogatz.com/books/the-joy-of-x
- Used: rock-pile / rearrange intuition for commutative arithmetic.
- Attribute as popular exposition / pedagogy, not primary historical scholarship.
